# PraxisLume Project Prompt

You are working on **PraxisLume**, a doctor-focused content, branding, and patient acquisition product.

The product should not be treated as a generic social media scheduler or a Canva clone. It should become a **Doctor Growth OS**: a tool that converts a doctor's clinical knowledge into patient-friendly branded content, clinic visibility, appointment enquiries, and measurable growth.

## Core Product Promise

Build PraxisLume around this promise:

> Turn a doctor's specialty, clinic details, services, brand identity, and local context into a 30-day patient acquisition campaign with posts, carousels, reel scripts, WhatsApp CTAs, clinic posters, and appointment links.

The wow factor is not "AI writes captions." The wow factor is:

> A doctor enters clinic profile, specialty, location, services, logo, tone, and brand colors. PraxisLume generates a usable monthly content and patient funnel system.

## Target Users

Primary early users:

- Solo doctors
- Small clinics
- Dentists
- Dermatologists
- ENT clinics
- Pediatricians
- Gynecologists and IVF clinics
- Physiotherapists
- Small specialty practices

The first strong wedge should be doctors who know they need content but do not have the time, design skill, or marketing team to produce it consistently.

## Strategic Positioning

PraxisLume should sit between:

- Canva
- Buffer/Later/social schedulers
- HeyGen/avatar tools
- Generic AI writing tools
- Healthcare marketing agencies

But it should be narrower and sharper:

> Agency-like clinic marketing output, generated in-house by the doctor or clinic team.

## Product Principles

1. **Do not build a full Canva clone in v0.1.**  
   Use structured templates, brand kits, and controlled editing.

2. **Do not make AI avatars the core product.**  
   Avatar/video generation should be a premium credit-based add-on later.

3. **Use AI for structure, not for everything.**  
   Generate campaign plans, captions, scripts, and slide content as structured JSON. Use deterministic rendering for branded assets.

4. **Keep medical content conservative.**  
   Include compliance checks, disclaimers, no cure guarantees, no unethical claims, no patient-identifiable data.

5. **Focus on patient acquisition, not vanity content.**  
   Every campaign should have a CTA, appointment path, WhatsApp message, or review loop.

6. **Build step by step.**  
   Do not one-shot the final product. Each version should be usable and demonstrable.

## Recommended Tech Stack

Preferred stack:

- **Frontend:** Flutter
- **Database/Auth/Storage:** Supabase
- **Vector Memory:** Supabase Postgres with pgvector
- **Backend AI Orchestrator:** Node/TypeScript API or Python/FastAPI
- **Template Rendering:** Server-side or Flutter-side deterministic rendering
- **Video Rendering Later:** FFmpeg worker / Remotion / Creatomate / Shotstack
- **AI Routing:** LiteLLM, Vercel AI Gateway, or provider-agnostic abstraction

Important architecture rule:

> Flutter is the body. Supabase is the memory. Templates are the hands. The AI orchestrator is the brain.

## AI Brain Architecture

PraxisLume should not depend on one single LLM. Build a model cascade:

- Cheap model for bulk drafts
- Premium model for final polish and medical safety review
- Embedding model for brand memory and retrieval
- Optional image model only when explicitly requested
- Voice/video APIs only through paid credits

Suggested brain modules:

- **Campaign Planner:** Creates 7/15/30-day plans by specialty, season, clinic goal, and local context.
- **Medical Copywriter:** Writes patient-friendly captions, carousel text, FAQs, and reel scripts.
- **Compliance Reviewer:** Removes unsafe claims, cure guarantees, fearmongering, and unethical wording.
- **Brand Stylist:** Applies tone, clinic identity, doctor profile, color palette, CTA, and disclaimers.
- **Template Renderer:** Converts structured content into carousels, posters, thumbnails, and overlays.
- **Funnel Engine:** Adds WhatsApp CTA, appointment links, review request flows, and landing-page copy.
- **Analytics Brain:** Learns which topics and formats bring enquiries.

## AI Cost Doctrine

Keep API costs under control from day one.

Use this approach:

- Captions, scripts, content ideas: included in subscription using cheap models.
- Carousels: template-rendered, not AI-image-generated by default.
- Premium rewrite/review: limited monthly usage.
- AI images: credit-based.
- Voice generation: credit-based.
- Avatar videos: paid add-on only.
- Bulk campaign generation: plan-limited.

Core rule:

> PraxisLume's wow factor should not depend on expensive AI. The core experience should work through cheap LLM calls plus excellent templates.

## Core Workflow

The ideal user journey:

1. Doctor signs up.
2. Doctor enters specialty, clinic name, location, services, qualifications, tone, and brand kit.
3. PraxisLume generates a 30-day campaign.
4. Doctor reviews and approves topics.
5. App generates carousels, captions, reel scripts, WhatsApp CTAs, Google Business posts, and posters.
6. Clinic exports or schedules content.
7. Enquiries are tracked manually or automatically.
8. Results feed future campaign recommendations.

## Compliance Guardrails

Medical marketing must be conservative.

Avoid:

- Cure guarantees
- Before/after claims without appropriate consent and rules
- Fear-based exaggeration
- Patient-identifiable stories
- Overpromising results
- Direct claims of being "best" without substantiation
- Misleading urgency
- Unverified medical claims

Include:

- General education disclaimer
- "Consult a qualified doctor" style language
- Doctor approval before publishing
- Consent reminder for patient images/testimonials
